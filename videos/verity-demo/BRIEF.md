## Intent

The product demo for the Verity landing page (verity.qashifthq.com) — the "show me
it working" moment TestSprite has and we lack. Sell the product by showing its real
flow, end to end: paste a URL → Verity opens the live app and writes readable
Playwright tests (UI, API, visual, AI-agent evals, load, security) → commit one
workflow file → every pull request gets a verdict with evidence; flaky is told apart
from a real failure; runs in your CI are unlimited; free plan to start. Confident,
precise, developer-credible — no hype, no stock imagery.

## Assets
